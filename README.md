# Retrofy

![image](retrofy.png)

Music client for Legacy iOS (Requires a premium account)

- iOS 6+
- armv7 and armv7s (iPhone 3GS and newer)
- Spotify playback, and Spotify Connect
- Album art
- Search
- Home, with Spotify's Made For You playlists
- Lyrics
- AI DJ, with its commentary as text
- Lock screen and control center

App icons made by u/AmadeoOOFDeReddit


You need your own client ID from the Spotify developer dashboard, with `http://127.0.0.1:8888/callback` as a redirect URI. Enter it on the login page Retrofy serves on your Wi-Fi.
